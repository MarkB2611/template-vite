,Penetration(how many enemies it can hit through before deletion),DepletionAmount(1 generally, burst weapons are different),BaseCost(cost from the weaponPickup),AssetDirectory(paretn directory of the "still" and "Anim" folders to provide basic animations and images for the wallbuys)
