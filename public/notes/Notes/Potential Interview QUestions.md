**SCENARIO‑BASED QUESTIONS (Hypothetical “What would you do…”)**
**Teamwork & Collaboration**
**A colleague disagrees with your approach in a meeting — 	what would you do.**
- Mediate via third party, gain insights outside of me and the other member

**You’re working with someone who is disengaged — how would 	you re‑engage them.**
- ask them if there are any tasks they are struggling with or becoming disinterested in - ask if a reallocation of tasks would be appropriate - or extra people to help
- failing that let them talk through the problem and see why it is makingthem disengaged - ask if any extra resources could help such as business requirements

**Your team is split on how to proceed with a task — how do 	you help them reach alignment.**
- Weigh up a pros and cons list of positives and negatives regarding either approach
- put forward my insights into what I believe is the best task while showing the merit and reasoning behind either approach

**You’re joining a new team with established ways of working 	— how do you integrate smoothly.**
- Ask for resources about the teams current methods of working and see how I would integrate to these methods.


**Stakeholder Management**
**A stakeholder gives unclear or conflicting requirements — 	how do you handle it.**
- Immediately ask for a meeting/summit over the reqauiremnts and ask clearly using non technical langauge regarding whatthe stakeholder wants
provide prototyping or diagrams/wireframes to help visualise the requirement if necessary(i.e frontend) 
provide real world examples if its regardingrhe backend
ask about the intended userflow.

**A senior stakeholder pushes for a solution you believe won’t 	work — what’s your approach.**
- Explain the real world outcomes of what may happen with that method - explain why a different method could be more appropriate
- Walk through why you are not confident with teh method they proposed while showing you understand their reasoning for wanting that approach - i.e scalability, maintainability, not relying on third party software etc
- Every method is somewhat valid it depends on the applications of the project and what is needed for that specific one.

**A stakeholder keeps changing their mind — how do you manage 	expectations.**
- Explain that the time required to implement changes this late is increased and the cost of changes have as well - the more changes later down the line costs the project more in resources
- Explain I will try my best to meet these changing business reuqiremnts but I am worried other areas could potentially suffer if we try to implement every change in requirement right away.

**You need information from a stakeholder who is slow to 	respond — what do you do.**
- Note down you tried to contact the stakeholder and in the mean time worked on the project the best I can with the existing information given
- Also try to improve the reusability and robustness so functions could be reused for different purposes speeding up the time it takes to make changes.
